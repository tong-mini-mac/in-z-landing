# Content Creator — User Manual

IN Z Content Creator is a Thai + SEA content platform: podcast pipelines and AI Video, with support for Thai dialects, English, Vietnamese, and Indonesian.

## Getting started

1. Sign in at [inz.lol](https://www.inz.lol/auth?mode=signin).
2. Open Content Creator from **Your package**.
3. Start with a 14-day free trial, then choose Starter, Creator, or Pro.

## Workflows

- Podcast: upload → AI transcription → show notes → clips → RSS
- AI Video: idea chat → script → media match → TTS → render MP4 → publish
