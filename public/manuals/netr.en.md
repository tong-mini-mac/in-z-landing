# NetR — User Manual

IN Z NetR maps partners, channels, and operating links so teams can keep counterparties, referral paths, and collaboration status in one place.

## Getting started

1. Sign in at [inz.lol](https://www.inz.lol/auth?mode=signin).
2. Open NetR from **Your package** when the product is available for your account.
3. Contact IN Z sales for Early Bird packaging if you do not yet have access.

## Purpose

Structured network visibility without building a custom CRM from scratch.
