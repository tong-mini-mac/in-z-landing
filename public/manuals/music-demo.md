# Music Demo — User Manual

IN Z Music Demo is an AI music creation studio. Generate drafts from prompts, preview takes, then export lyrics, chords, MIDI, MusicXML, and optional AI vocal preview.

## Getting started

1. Sign in at [inz.lol](https://www.inz.lol/auth?mode=signin).
2. Open Music Demo from **Your package**.
3. Signup includes free credits. Create a song costs 50 credits; regenerate costs 25.
